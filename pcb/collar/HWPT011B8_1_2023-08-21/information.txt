Manufacturing Formats.
----------------------

Gerber Format
-------------
Output Format:-   RS274-X
Units:-           Metric
Digits :-         3(Leading),4(Trailing)
Co-Ordinates:-    Absolute
Zero Suppress:-   Leading
Suppress Repeat:- Yes

Excellon Drill Formats
----------------------
Output Format:-   ASCII
Units:-           Imperial/English
Digits :-         2(Leading),4(Trailing)
Co-Ordinates:-    Absolute
Zero Suppress:-   Trailing
Suppress Repeat:- Yes
